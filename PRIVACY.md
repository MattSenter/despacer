# Despacer Privacy Policy

**English** · [Deutsch](PRIVACY.de.md) · [Español](PRIVACY.es.md) · [Français](PRIVACY.fr.md) · [日本語](PRIVACY.ja.md) · [한국어](PRIVACY.ko.md) · [Português (Brasil)](PRIVACY.pt-BR.md) · [简体中文](PRIVACY.zh-Hans.md) · [繁體中文](PRIVACY.zh-Hant.md)

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Despacer collects no data. None.

- It makes no network connections.
- It has no account, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking.
- It reads the clipboard only right after you copy something while a terminal app (one listed in its Terminals settings) is in front. Copies from every other app are never read.
- To find the exact original of a copied command, it reads the session files that Claude Code and Codex keep on your computer (`~/.claude/projects` and `~/.codex/sessions`). It only reads them. It never changes, copies, or uploads them. You can turn this off with **Match session history**.
- It keeps your last ten fixes in memory so you can undo them. They are never written to disk and are gone when Despacer quits.
- Its settings (which features are on, which apps count as terminals, Show in menu bar, Open at login) are saved on your computer only.

If this ever changes, this page will say so first.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/MattSenter/despacer/issues.
